# FrankenOCR Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

FrankenOCR is designed to recognize documents and understand images locally on your
device. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking, and the
FrankenOCR developer does not collect personal data from the app.

## Data processed on your device

- Images and scanned PDFs you choose from Photos or Files are read and processed only
  on your device.
- Photos you take with the in-app camera are processed only on your device.
- Questions you enter for image description are sent only to the model running on your
  device.
- Recognized text, Markdown, HTML, chart data, and MusicXML remain on your device unless
  you explicitly copy them or use the system share sheet.
- Downloaded model files are stored in the app's private storage and excluded from
  device backups. You can remove a downloaded model from within the app.

FrankenOCR does not upload selected images, camera photos, documents, questions, or
recognition results.

## Network access

With your consent, the app downloads the model files you choose from the FrankenOCR
project's Hugging Face repository or GitHub Releases. These requests do not contain
your images, documents, questions, or recognition results. The hosting provider may
receive routine network request information, such as an IP address and user agent,
under its own privacy policy. The FrankenOCR developer does not receive this request
information from the app.

## Permissions

- Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a picture in FrankenOCR.
  You can deny or revoke this permission in iOS Settings and continue importing files.
- Importing from Photos uses Apple's system photo picker, which gives FrankenOCR access
  only to the pictures you select rather than to your full photo library.
- Importing from Files uses Apple's system document picker, which gives FrankenOCR
  access only to the files you select.

## Children

Because FrankenOCR does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect
personal data from children.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be published in this document with a new effective date. For
privacy questions, open an issue in the
[FrankenOCR repository](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/franken_ocr/issues).
